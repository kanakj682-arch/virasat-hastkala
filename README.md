# Virasat - Hastkala (Craft to Market Platform)

An AI-powered digital platform designed to bridge the gap between Indian artisans and market buyers. The platform empowers traditional craftspeople to easily digitize their products using voice/photo workflows and connects them directly with corporate, bulk, and retail buyers without middlemen.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎙️ For Artisans
- **Voice-First & Multilingual Interface:** Artisans can describe their products using simple voice notes in their regional language (Hindi, English, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, etc.).
- **Instant AI Catalogue Creation:** Upload a photo or speak to automatically generate clean, professional product pages with descriptions, estimated pricing, and regional tags.
- **Direct UPI Payments:** Ensures ~90% of sales proceeds go directly to the artisan's account.
- **Low Digital Literacy Friendly:** Simple, minimal UI built specifically for ease of use.

### 💼 For Buyers
- **Market Opportunity Matching:** AI matches buyer briefs (e.g., corporate Diwali gifting) with artisan capacities and crafts with up to 94% accuracy.
- **GI Tag & Eco-Friendly Verification:** Filter and source genuine, eco-friendly, and GI-verified Indian handicrafts directly from artisans across regions (Jaipur Blue Pottery, Chanderi Silk, Brass Work, Jute Crafts, etc.).
- **Artisan Portal & Buyer Dashboard:** Dedicated portals for both buyers to submit requirements and artisans to manage inventory/orders.
